How Incident Hub works

From submitting a report to checking its progress, the process is straightforward.

01

Create your account

Register using your account details, then log in to access the student portal.

02

Submit a report

Describe the incident, provide its location, and select the category and severity.

03

Track the progress

Check the status of your reports while authorized staff review and respond.

SAFETY AND RESPONSIBILITY

A report should be handled with care.

Incident Hub is designed to help the school receive and manage student concerns. Report information should only be accessed by authorized personnel and handled responsibly throughout the response process.
